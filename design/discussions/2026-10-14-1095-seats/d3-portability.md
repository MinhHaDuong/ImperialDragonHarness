**Liveness:** use `live / unknown / confirmed exited`, with supporting evidence. Open descriptors, cwd and session IDs in process metadata provide positive signals; their absence does not prove death. The existing Claude scanner silently skips inaccessible process entries. My process-name probe also found no history descriptor for the visible Codex process, illustrating why that signal alone fails. Reliable exit claims need launch identity—host, PID plus process start time, parent/session ID—and an exit observation. Without it, report unknown. Dead creators can leave live descendants or other consumers.

Label path mentions, attempted commands, successful creation and inferred test provenance separately. Candidate-directory mtime is an incomplete search bound; later activity can hide the original trace. Bound effort explicitly and mark incomplete searches. Citation searches likewise cannot certify “uncited.”
