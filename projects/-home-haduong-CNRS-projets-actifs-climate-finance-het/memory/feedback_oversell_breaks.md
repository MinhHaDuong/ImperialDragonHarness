The mid-2010s JS divergence signal (elevated z-scores 2013–2016 across all window sizes) is real, not noise. The censored-gap technique suppresses it by design — that's a methodological choice, not a finding.

**Why:** The NCC epic (0012) was killed because "Paris didn't matter" oversold what the data shows. The Oeconomia paper's framing ("corroborates" a historically-grounded periodization) is honest and defensible. The companion paper's framing ("endogenous break detection contradicts COP-milestone chronology") is not.

**How to apply:** When writing about break detection results, always frame computation as corroborative of historical narrative, never as the primary evidence. Acknowledge the mid-2010s signal honestly. The censored gap is a tool, not proof.
