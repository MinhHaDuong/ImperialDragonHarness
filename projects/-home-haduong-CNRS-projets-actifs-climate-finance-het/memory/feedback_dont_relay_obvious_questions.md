Filter executor questions before they reach the author. Decide yourself anything the documents, public facts or the author's own published material settle, record the rule, and move on.

**Why:** on 2026-09-23 I relayed "is 'Government of Indonesia and International Partners Group' one publisher?" ("Are you really asking…?") and asked the author to write a "Who we are" text when his homepage and CVs were on disk ("what are you asking me to write text?"). Both cost his attention for nothing.

**How to apply:** before asking, check whether a plain rule decides it ("X via Y" → publisher X; two organisations → joint publishers) or a local source supplies it (~/CNRS/html/src for bio). Ask only for choices that change meaning or intent.
