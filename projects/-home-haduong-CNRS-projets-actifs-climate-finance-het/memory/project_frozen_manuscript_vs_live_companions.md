The clean-room writing build (0131/0163/0207) worked for the **manuscript** only
because the manuscript is a **frozen submission**: `content/manuscript-vars.yml`
is pinned to v1.0 values and explicitly *not* generated by `compute_vars.py`; its
3 figures + `content/tables/tab_venues.md` are git-tracked byte-stable deliverables.

The four companion papers are **not symmetric** — do not assume they mirror the
manuscript:

- `technical-report.qmd`, `data-paper.qmd`, `multilayer-detection.qmd`,
  `corpus-report.qmd`
- Their `*-vars.yml` are **live** `compute_vars.py` outputs from `$(REFINED)`,
  emitted together via a grouped `&:` target (`COMPUTED_STATS` in the Makefile).
- Their figures/tables are live `plot_*` / `export_*` outputs.
- **None are git-tracked** — only the shared `content/_includes/*.md` are.

**Consequence for any "make paper X clean-room" work:** to make a companion build
data-less, its `vars.yml` must be pulled OUT of the `compute_vars.py` grouped
target and pinned (like `manuscript-vars.yml`), and its figures/tables committed
via `.gitignore` negations. That **freezes** the numbers — they stop tracking the
corpus until deliberately re-frozen. Correct for a submission (freeze at
submission); a silent drift hazard for a live internal report. Pair each freeze
with a freshness guard (cf. 0205's ratchet-freshness guard) so a stale freeze
can't pass.

Author decision (2026-07-09, [[feedback_atomic_tickets_validation_units]]): freeze
all four anyway, staged, **data-paper first** (clearest submission case, RDJ4HSS).
Recorded in ticket 0207. Caught before writing build code by read-first inspection
([[feedback_pilot_one_instance_critiques_the_ticket]]).
