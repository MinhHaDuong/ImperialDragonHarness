Research Data Journal for the Humanities and Social Sciences (RDJ4HSS):
- Publisher: IISG (International Institute of Social History), Amsterdam
- Platform: platform.openjournals.nl/RDJHSS (moved from Brill in 2025)
- Diamond OA, CC BY 4.0, Scopus + ERIH PLUS + DOAJ indexed
- E-ISSN: 2452-3666

Data paper format (from Petram & Kruizinga 2024 exemplar):
- Max 2,500 words body
- Sections: Abstract, Keywords, Related dataset (DOI), 1. Introduction, 2. Method (2.1 Sources, 2.2 Data Structure, 2.3 Quality/Biases), 3. Data, 4. Concluding Remarks, Acknowledgements, References
- Author-date citation style (APA-like)
- Figures and tables allowed (exemplar had 2 figures, 3 tables, 10 pages)
- Numbered sections (1., 2., 2.1, etc.)
- Dataset must be in a trusted repository (Zenodo qualifies)
- Reviewed by peer reviewers AND data specialists
