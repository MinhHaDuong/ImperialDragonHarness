Post-submission paper seed, spun out of ticket 0166 (closed 2026-07-09 — this
seed is the paper's durable entry point, so 0166 did not linger open).

**Thesis**: policy *instrument* concepts in the climate-finance corpus circulate
institutional/practice→academia, while theoretical frames circulate the other
way — the scientization asymmetry of Hirschman & Popp Berman (2014). A full
six-term lead-lag study (Rio markers, grant equivalent, mobilised private
finance, additionality, blended finance, de-risking) would be a contribution to
scientization / STS studies of economics in its own right.

**Pilot evidence (de-risking, 2026-07-09)**: 98 corpus hits, 2012→2025. Earliest
attestations are institutional — CPI 2012 "Moving the Fulcrum" primer, UNDP DREI
~2013 — before the *Nature Climate Change* academic framing in 2014. Content
corroborates the thesis.

**Why it left the R&R**: the corpus's `from_grey` boolean (208 rows, ~0.7%,
median year 2021) is blind to early institutional documents — OpenAlex indexes
grey literature and files it as academic. Detecting lead-lag needs a
**venue-based segmentation** (working-paper servers, institutional repositories,
IGO/NGO report series → grey; peer-reviewed journals → academic), which is a
methodological project, not a script against an existing flag. Wrong bet against
the R&R clock (gated on 0172 manuscript rebuild), right bet as a standalone paper.

For the R&R itself, de-risking is used as a *named narrative* for referee remark
R1.1 (economists as policy professionals; econ-journal under-representation is a
finding, not a selection artifact) — see 0166 Findings note and
`docs/0152-coverage-matrix.md` R1.1 row.
