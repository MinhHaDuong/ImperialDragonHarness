Œconomia (editor Thomas Delcey) rejected "History of climate finance as an economic
object: accounting categories, controversies, and economists as policy experts"
definitively on 2026-09-16, and asked the author not to submit "this type of work"
to the journal again. Letter: `~/CNRS/papiers/actif/Inventing_Climate_Finance/2026-07-21 Oeconomia revision/20260916170722_[Œconomia] Editor Decision on on your submission.pdf`.

The editor's grounds: the revision read as a filter applied to generated output
(condensed, "rebuilt", prose-checked by the build) rather than personal engagement;
prose "jargon-heavy and falsely sophisticated"; and a methodological contradiction —
the introduction justifies computation because no single reader can master the
literature, while Appendix A.1 presents the computation as merely confirming a
historical record the author already established, without describing that
qualitative protocol.

**Why:** any plan premised on an Œconomia revision round is void, and the critique
applies to the next venue too.

**How to apply:** never route findings to "the Œconomia revision"; do not propose
Œconomia as a venue for this line of work. When the article is reworked for another
journal, the two substantive objections (the computation-vs-qualitative contradiction,
undescribed qualitative protocol) must be answered in the text, and how AI assistance
is described to editors is the author's call, not a build detail to volunteer.
