On 2026-09-23 Minh sent Gavard and Schoch (reply in the existing thread, from minh@haduong.com)
three verified data errors: the 40% significant-marker weighting is stated but not applied, the two
Congos' Rio finance is swapped, and Table A2 is labelled mitigation but shows total climate. The
dual-marked overlap was offered as an optional disclosure. The letter suggests an author-filed
corrigendum to EDE, announces the comment, and asks for a reply by 2026-10-21. Copies are in
`correspondence/sent-2026-09-23/`; the evidence is in RN-011 of `ReproductibilityNotes.md`.

**Why:** errors that barely move the results still misdescribe the data. The fair and effective
channel is for the authors to correct them, which leaves the comment free to argue substance: the
2013 provider-reporting break, and what the design identifies. The EDE editor is contacted only if
the authors do not respond.

**How to apply:**
- Do not put the bookkeeping errors at the centre of the comment. Cite the corrigendum if it exists.
- Keep the literature review (REL) out of correspondence with these authors unless it adds something
  new about their paper.
- The data note (`data-note/`, approved by Minh 2026-09-23) goes to them first, and on Zenodo after
  their reply or after 2026-10-21.
- Pre-2013 gap filling and post-2017 extension of the data belong to the REL paper, not to the comment.
