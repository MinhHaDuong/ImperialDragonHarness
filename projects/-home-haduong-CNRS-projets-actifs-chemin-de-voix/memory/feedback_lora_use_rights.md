**Why:** No law prohibits training a LoRA on books you own for personal/research use. Copyright restrictions in the project concern *public redistribution* (e.g., FR writings of HCM on Gallica under Gallica terms), not local model training.

**How to apply:** Do not raise copyright/use-rights objections for materials already present in `voix-*/raw/`. The relevant restriction is *public-domain for web scraping new sources* (fetch only PD or freely-licensed content from the internet); *owned books already on disk* are unrestricted for this project's training purposes. TNH corpus (raw/ has purchased EPUBs/PDFs) is usable under this principle.
