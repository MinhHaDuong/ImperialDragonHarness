Corrections Minh gave on billet nº2 and its LinkedIn text, 2026-09-17:

- **No contrast farming.** "X cannot; Y can", "not X but Y", "stops being X
  and becomes Y" all read as machine padding to him, even when the global
  rule's exact pattern is avoided. State the positive form and stop.
- **Bullets are self-contained paragraphs.** Each starts with its own action
  or idea, never with "It"; a reader who sees only that item must
  understand it.
- **No numbers or labels without context in a promo text.** "The
  thirteen-line template" means nothing to a LinkedIn reader who has not
  read the post; say "the file to copy".
- **Idioms verbatim.** A wink on a proverb keeps the proverb's exact words
  ("Never say never", not "Never write never").
- **No clickbait hook.** "Learned the hard way" promises an anecdote the
  text does not deliver; he called it putaclic. The hook is the fact.
- **One literary archaism per post, chosen** (rule now in the blog's
  backlog); a reviewer may flag it, must not correct it.
- **Check the technical claim before the style.** "Claude Code reads
  AGENTS.md" slipped into the LinkedIn text after the post had it right;
  he caught it, not the reviewers' first pass.

**Why:** posts written with AI about AI must be irreproachable on machine
tics, and the promo text is read by the same peers.
**How to apply:** proofread promo texts sentence by sentence before showing
them; run the three-reader review after, not instead. See
[[reference-offinity]] for the diary register.
