When drafting a LinkedIn post, always lead with a short personal note — why the
user is sharing this, their connection to it, or who in their network it's
relevant for — before the factual summary of the linked content.

**Why:** User confirmed this as a standing preference ("Always a personal
note") after being asked whether to add one to a job-posting share draft.

**How to apply:** For any future LinkedIn post draft, open with 1-2 sentences
of personal framing tailored to the specific content (e.g., relevance to the
user's own research area, a personal connection to the institution/project,
or why the audience should care) before the informational body. If the
specific personal angle isn't known, ask the user rather than inventing
unconfirmed personal facts (a fabricated connection is worse than a placeholder
asking for one).
