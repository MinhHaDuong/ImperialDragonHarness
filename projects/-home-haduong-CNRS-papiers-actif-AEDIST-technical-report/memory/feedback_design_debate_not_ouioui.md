When asked to place a figure or restructure a presentation, do NOT just agree to the first proposal. Spin 3 independent agents to debate the design from different perspectives before committing.

**Why:** User said "ouioui" (you just agreed without challenging) when the agent accepted a figure placement without questioning whether it was optimal. The 3-agent debate (when eventually run) surfaced a real design tension (advance organizer vs audience familiarity) and produced rationale for each decision.

**How to apply:** Any slide ordering decision, section restructure, or figure placement proposal → challenge it first. If it's genuinely non-obvious, run the multi-agent debate. Don't default to agreement on design questions.
