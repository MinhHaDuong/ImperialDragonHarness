During the 0522 editorial round (2026-06-10), the author asked in-session whether
the equalisation finding was "safe to defend" given it is common knowledge among
RAG experts. The agent added a defensive sentence to the manuscript ("The
direction accords with practitioner experience…"). The author corrected
immediately: "that was for your eyes only, not to add to the text. And I would
never write accord with."

**Why:** In-session questions are the author thinking aloud / probing the agent's
judgment — answering them belongs in the conversation, not the deliverable.
Inserting them into prose is scope creep into an author-blessed text.

**How to apply:** (1) When the author asks an evaluative question mid-edit,
answer in chat; touch the manuscript only if he says so. (2) Voice: Minh never
writes formal-cliché verbs like "accord with"; prefer plain ones ("matches",
"is consistent with", "bears this out"). Related: [[project-tactical-editorial-round-0522]].
