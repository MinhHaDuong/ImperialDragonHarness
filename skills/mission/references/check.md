# check

The talk is days away. Steps 1-6 run in order; 5 stops for the author.

## Registers

- Roadmap: the row's status moves to "ready" once the slides are sent.
- Publications list: no.

## Provenance

The slides sent to the organisers are the version of record for the talk:
file that exact PDF in the dossier's production folder, hashed, before any
last-minute edit.

## Filesystem

1. Session brief, one page in the dossier: day, slot, room, chair,
   co-speakers, announced title, time allowed, the organisers' contact.
   Taken from the programme and the mails, each line with its source.
2. Talking points beside the slides: the three things the audience should
   leave with, and the answer to the obvious question.
3. Bag list, paper and electronic: travel order and bookings at hand
   (`voyage/`), slides on two media plus a copy the author uploads to their own
   online storage, adapter and clicker,
   receipts envelope, badge or registration proof.

4. Contacts to meet, read-only: the agent looks each one up in the author's
   directory, where one is installed, and lists them in the brief with what
   to discuss.

## Outward gates (who clicks)

5. Slides sent to the organisers and the chair the day before: the agent
   drafts the mail with the PDF attached; the author sends it.
6. Roadmap row moved to "ready": the agent edits the local roadmap file once
   the slides are sent.

## Delegates

- `/slides` to build or review the deck; `/message-framing` if the message
  is still unsettled.
- `/pdf-finish` for the slides PDF that leaves the machine.

## Author-only (ask, do not drop)

- Rehearsal timed against the slot.
- Which version of the slides goes public later (page, open archive), if any.

## Done when

The brief, talking points and bag list are in the dossier, the slides are
sent and filed with their hash, and every author-only item has an answer.
