## Part 1: Summary (~50% of note)

### Research Question and Approach

What question does this work address? What approach or method does it employ?

### Main Arguments or Findings

What are the core claims or results? (3-5 key points maximum)

### Methods and Data

What evidence base or analytical approach? How is the analysis conducted?

### Key Concepts and Theoretical Framework

What theoretical resources are mobilized? What concepts are central?

---

## Part 2: Critical Analysis (~50% of note)

### Strengths

What does this work do well? What contributions does it make?

### Limitations

What are the gaps, oversights, or weak points? What is left unexamined?

### Position in Larger Debates

How does this relate to other work in the field? Which scholarly conversations does it join?

### Assumptions and Naturalized Elements

**Explicit assumptions:**
What does the author acknowledge as starting points or givens?

**Implicit assumptions:**
What is taken for granted without discussion?

**Naturalized elements:**
What is presented as self-evident or technical when it might be contingent or political?

### Theoretical or Methodological Innovations

What is new or distinctive about the approach? What tools or frameworks are introduced?

### Gaps and Blind Spots

What questions are not asked? What perspectives are absent? What data or cases are not examined?

---

## Part 3: Relevance to Your Research

### Connection to Research Question

How does this article relate to your guiding question? Does it:
- Provide theoretical framework?
- Offer methodological model?
- Present empirical precedent?
- Represent position in controversy?

### Analytical Axis Informed

Which of your analytical axes does this illuminate?

### Controversy Clarified

What debate or tension does this help map?

### Gaps This Reveals

Does this work reveal lacunae in the literature that your research could address?
