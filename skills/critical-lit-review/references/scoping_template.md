# Scoping Document Template

Flexible template for delimiting a scientific object in environmental economics, energy policy, decision science, or related fields.

## Document Structure

```markdown
# Scoping Document: [Title of Scientific Object]

**Author:** [Your name]
**Date:** [Date]
**Research Domain:** [Environmental economics / Energy policy / Decision science / Risk assessment / etc.]

## 1. Scientific Object Definition

[2-3 sentence precise definition of what you're studying. NOT just a theme.]

**Example formats by domain:**

Environmental economics:
"Barriers to renewable energy adoption in Vietnam's power sector, analyzed through economic and socio-technical frameworks, covering policy instruments, cost structures, and institutional factors, 2010-2025."

Decision under uncertainty:
"Methods for aggregating expert judgments under deep uncertainty in climate risk assessment, comparing Bayesian, Dempster-Shafer, and possibilistic approaches, with applications to climate sensitivity estimation."

Technology assessment:
"Public perception and acceptability of carbon capture and storage (CCS) in France, examining risk perceptions, trust in institutions, and information effects through surveys and focus groups, 2007-2012."
```
