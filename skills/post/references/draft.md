# draft

The post or op-ed is being written. This stage only delegates: it settles
the text and changes nothing outside the post's folder.

## Registers

- Roadmap: yes, once the piece is decided: a row in the communications
  section as the local `AGENTS.md` names it, status "in progress".
- Publications list: no, not before publication.

## Provenance

The text as validated by the author, or as sent to the venue for an op-ed:
saved in the post's folder under a name that says so, before any later edit.

## Filesystem

1. The working Markdown in the post's folder, named as the local `AGENTS.md`
   says. It stays the source of truth for every later stage.

## Outward gates (who clicks)

None: nothing leaves the machine at this stage. Sending an op-ed to an
editor is the author's act, recorded at the publish stage.

## Delegates

- `/message-framing` to settle the message, then `/cut-prose` to the venue's
  length.
- Editorial rules (language per post, recurring themes, red lines,
  declassification of incidents) are read from the project's backlog or
  `AGENTS.md`, not copied here.

## Author-only (ask, do not drop)

- Language and title.
- Sign-off on the text.
- For an op-ed, which venue it goes to.

## Done when

The validated text is in the post's folder and every author-only item has an
answer.
