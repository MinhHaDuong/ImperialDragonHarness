# publish

The text is settled and goes live by one channel: step 1 (own blog) or step
2 (newspaper or partner site), each stopping for the author; then steps 3 and
4 record what went live. A post already live (its URL answers with the final
text) skips to step 3; it is done once steps 3 and 4 have their evidence.

## Registers

- Roadmap: the row's status moves to "published", with the live URL.
- Publications list: not at this stage; the archive stage indexes it.

## Provenance

The live page, fetched after publication, is the version of record. For an
op-ed, keep the version sent, the version published, and the diff between
them.

## Outward gates (who clicks)

Own blog:

1. The publishing recipe stays in the blog's `AGENTS.md`; follow it there.
   Gates: the post is created as a draft, category and tags set, the author
   previews it; publication is the author's gesture. After any edit in the
   platform's own editor, fetch the content again and diff it against the
   local copy before publishing.

Newspaper or partner site:

2. The editor publishes. The author sends the text; the agent saves the
   version sent and records the date.

## Filesystem

3. The live page, fetched once it is up, saved beside the source Markdown
   (the rendered page and, where the platform gives one, its API record).
4. For an op-ed, the sent and published versions side by side, with their
   diff as a short note: what the editor changed.

## Delegates

- The mail skill, where installed, for a cover note to an editor; otherwise
  the author sends.

## Author-only (ask, do not drop)

- Publication clicked (own blog) or the text sent (op-ed).
- Diffusion on social networks and to colleagues.
- For an op-ed, whether the editor's changes are accepted.

## Done when

The post is live, its live page is saved, an op-ed's sent-versus-published
diff is noted, and every author-only item has an answer.
