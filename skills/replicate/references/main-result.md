# Main-result choice

Choose the original paper's central substantive claim before package inspection
or execution. Work sequentially: establish the claim, identify its supporting
table or figure, record the choice, then inspect the package. Package availability,
software convenience, or a successful secondary result cannot choose the target.

Read the original abstract and introduction together. Record the central claim's
authoritative passages and exact article version in `README.md`, then identify
the main table or figure that supports it and the relevant panels/models. Retain
any uncertainty about the claim-to-output link. If authoritative original text
cannot support a choice, leave `main_result` null with the paper blocker under
`assessment.md`; independently inspected package facts remain usable within their
coverage limits.

When present, inspect article-specific Highlights/Key findings on the official
original-article page and crosscheck them against the abstract, introduction,
and main supporting evidence. Availability varies; do not infer that every recent
article from a publisher has them. Record the page/version, relevant passages,
and agreement or conflict. Highlights corroborate or expose a conflict; they do
not silently override the original text. Missing or inaccessible highlights stay
an explicit source gap and need not block an otherwise supported choice.

Apply the validation source boundary before opening the page or briefing an
advisor. In blind mode use only the supplied original-paper route and permitted
direct metadata/full text; a publisher page may itself expose excluded replication
answers. Do not open an answer-bearing route, search for missing highlights, or
retrieve prior reports/notes to settle selection. Record inaccessible or excluded
material and pass the same exclusions and exposure record to helpers. An absent
permitted route authorizes no discovery.

Use proportionate route-advisor support for pivotal judgment: ambiguous centrality,
a disputed claim-to-output link, or material conflict among original text,
highlights, and supporting evidence. Give the advisor the permitted source evidence
and record its advice, disagreement, exposure limits, and the driver's reasoned
disposition. Advice is not scientific signoff or permission for additional sources,
new estimates, spending, or changed approval authority.

If results are equally central, the driver records a motivated choice prospectively,
before package inspection or execution, under a stated rule applied consistently
across the cohort. Record the candidates, evidence for equal centrality, choice
rule, and reason for the chosen supporting output. Package feasibility cannot
break the tie. A remaining unresolved pivotal judgment stays explicit rather than
being silently settled by whichever code runs.

Retain the original selection when its inputs are missing or a secondary output
runs; report the main-result blocker and secondary evidence separately. This rule
changes no comparison tolerance, ACRe criterion, or whole-paper coverage meaning.
Explicit selected-target requests remain additive under `assessment.md`: they do
not replace the canonical main result or its evidence. Preserve prior case records;
this prospective guidance authorizes no automatic historical reselection or rescore.
