# Validation exposure and calibration

Apply the skill's exposure check before opening case answers or delegating
retrieval. Record the run purpose, people/helper contexts exposed, sources
already read, and which papers or cohorts informed the rules under evaluation.
A report hidden during execution does not make a development paper fresh.

## Calibration and unused targets

When paper outputs help choose an implicit implementation convention, record
it as calibration. Before evaluating unused targets, commit a dated freeze
identifying the calibration tables/statistics, alternatives tried, chosen
conventions and reasons, and the targets not used for those choices. Bind it
to the code, inputs, environment, and comparison rules with their identifiers
and hashes. Preserve earlier choices and results rather than rewriting them.

Evaluate the unused targets under the frozen choices and report their results
separately from calibration matches. Record any subsequent inspection,
adjustment, or target reselection as post-hoc, with its reason and a new freeze
before another evaluation. An output used to choose or revise a convention is
no longer unused for that choice. If choices or targets were fixed only after
seeing results, disclose that timing; a later freeze cannot make it prospective.
These records use the existing printed-digit tolerance and scope declarations.
They do not decide held translation/scoring questions or authorize new probes.

## Fresh-paper validation

Unused tables within one paper share its data, estimators, and development
history. Their agreement checks transfer within that paper; it does not
establish independent performance on fresh papers or cohorts. Freeze the
rules, selection procedure, targets, and comparison protocol before evaluating
a fresh validation set. Exclude papers and development cohorts that informed
those rules, and disclose prior exposure and any post-hoc changes. Keep case
answers from executors, helpers, and plan signers until outputs are frozen.
Unknown or newly discovered exposure limits the validation claim; preserve the
results as disclosed reproduction/development evidence instead.

Weschle- and Atanasov-derived development evidence is excluded from fresh
validation of the resulting rules, as required by Replicator ticket 0004
(`tickets/0004-held-out-benchmark-of-replicate-on-about.erg` at commit
`a0bac78e9a8dac4647786822f2395cfcfad6d2b0`). The Gavard mapping is likewise
exposed calibration evidence. These cases remain usable for ordinary
reproduction or appraisal with disclosure. Paper/cohort eligibility and
exposure are separate from a within-paper unused-table split; report both.
