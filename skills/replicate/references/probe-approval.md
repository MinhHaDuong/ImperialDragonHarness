# Challenge-plan approval

The current command is challenge; this contractual reference path is retained.
There is no signature timeout: refusal, silence, or elapsed time never supplies
approval, including when a later request asks to complete. Compile the available
local report and retain the pending plan/dispositions while blocked.

Before any robustness execution, retain an approval record in
`robustness/plan.md` identifying the exact plan path, version or content hash,
and date. Approval covers that identified plan; a driver supplies scope and
provenance and cannot grant itself authority. Use one of these two modes.

## Author approval

The author may approve the identified plan in its file or in conversation.
Preserve an actual author-written `Signed-off-by: <name>, <YYYY-MM-DD>` line
as such; agents never write that line on the author's behalf. For approval in
conversation, or another author-written approval without that line, an agent
may record an explicitly labelled **approval transcription**. Retain the
approver's identity, approval date, exact plan/version, decision and conditions,
source conversation/message reference and relevant author words, and the
transcriber's identity and transcription date. Preserve the source or a durable
reference to it. A transcription reports an existing approval; it is neither
an author-written signature nor a means to infer approval from silence,
execution instructions for another plan, or an agent's paraphrase alone.
If the source does not identify which plan was approved, return to the author.

## Bounded campaign delegation

The author may explicitly delegate plan approval to a panel for a defined
campaign and fixed perimeter. Retain the actual author delegation or its
truthful, source-bearing transcription under the preceding provenance rules.
Record the campaign identifier, authorized panel or panel-selection rule,
perimeter (covered work and permitted changes), duration or end condition,
approval decision rule, and author conditions. Unspecified authority remains
with the author; incomplete delegation cannot be filled in by a driver.

For each plan/version, retain the panel opinions, disagreements, approval
decision under the delegated rule, conditions and evidence of their fulfilment,
and the panel approval record with identities and date. A majority or unanimous
vote has authority only when the author delegated that decision rule. Unresolved
conditions block execution. The panel cannot extend its own perimeter, renew
its mandate, or turn campaign approval into permanent authority.

## Amendments and boundaries

Preserve every plan amendment, its reason and changed version/hash, and its
approval before executing the changed plan. The author may approve amendments
directly. A delegated panel may approve them only within its existing perimeter
and mandate; changes outside it return to the author. Earlier approval never
silently transfers to a changed plan.

Both modes retain the inventory, fairness, scope, and reporting requirements
in `rules.md` and the skill's validation-exposure boundary. Record exposure of
panel approvers as well as author approvers; blind validation keeps answers out
of all approval contexts until results are frozen. Approval authorizes only
the identified empirical checks, subject to other applicable permissions. It
adds no pipeline, publication, author contact, paid-spend, comparison, or scoring
authority. Keep campaign names, drivers, projects, cohorts, and model choices
in the local plan and delegation record rather than in this generic policy.
